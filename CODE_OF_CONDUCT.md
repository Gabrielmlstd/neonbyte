# Code of Conduct

We want Neonbyte to be a welcoming place for beginners and experts alike.

**Do:** be respectful, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, credit other people's work, and respect the licenses of the projects you share.

**Don't:** harass or insult anyone, publish other people's private information, post malware, cracks or stolen material, or use the project to target individuals.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and listings that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately (see the Security tab or the contact address in the Terms page of your instance).
