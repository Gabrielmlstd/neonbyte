# Content policy

Neonbyte is a **discovery library for open source software**. It links to official sources and never hosts files.

## What can be listed

- Software released under an **open source license** (MIT, GPL, LGPL, AGPL, Apache, BSD, MPL, CC-BY-SA for content, and similar) with a public source repository.
- **Reverse engineering tools**: disassemblers, decompilers, debuggers, hex and binary editors, emulators, binary-analysis and instrumentation frameworks, file-format and protocol analyzers, CTF and education tools.
- **Re-implementations and open engines** that do not ship copyrighted assets (for example engines that require the user's own copy of the original game).
- Open source games, utilities, media and developer tools.
- Dual-use security tools, when they are openly licensed, documented and maintained for legitimate research or defense.

## What is not allowed

- Cracks, keygens, license or DRM bypasses, and patches whose purpose is to defeat protection of proprietary software.
- Leaked or stolen source code, firmware, assets, credentials or data.
- Malware, ransomware, stealers, botnets, or exploit kits built to attack systems their authors do not own.
- Software without a clear open source license, or with a missing or misleading license.
- Projects that target, dox or harass individuals.
- Direct download links to files hosted by the submitter that are not the project's official release page.

## How listings are handled

- Projects are published immediately and reviewed afterwards. Anyone can report a listing from its page.
- Reported listings are checked by the admins and removed if they break this policy.
- Owners of the software or of copyrighted material can request removal through the Content Removal page.
- Every listing shows a notice: visitors must research the project and download at their own risk.
